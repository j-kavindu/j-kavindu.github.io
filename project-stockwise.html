Stockwise

Make inventory costing visible, from stock movements to closing value.

Working MVPReactTypeScriptdecimal.jsFirebase-ready
Open demo ↗Public source code

The problem

What the project addresses

FIFO and weighted-average methods can produce different inventory and cost-of-sales results. Linked returns and negative stock make the workflow harder to explain.

The approach

A practical workflow

A costing app that connects purchases, sales and linked returns with stock cards and a comparison of periodic and perpetual valuation methods.

  • FIFO and weighted average under periodic and perpetual systems
  • Linked purchase and sales returns with quantity checks
  • Stock cards, low-stock alerts and CSV/JSON export

Subject & technology

What it demonstrates

Inventory valuation · accounting controls · decimal arithmetic.

Project stack: React, TypeScript, decimal.js, Firebase-ready.

Evidence & scope

Verification and limitations

Source, costing policies and verification notes inspected. The repository records 26 valuation tests and a desktop deployment smoke check; not a financial-compliance certification.

A single-period, single-warehouse costing MVP using synthetic LKR data. Public demo data stays in local storage. Live cloud acceptance and full mobile testing remain pending.
