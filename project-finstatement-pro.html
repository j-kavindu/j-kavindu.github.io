The problem

What the project addresses

Preparing statements involves more than importing a trial balance. Accounts, adjustments and equity movements need to reconcile before a report can be reviewed.

The approach

A practical workflow

An interactive reporting workflow for importing balances, mapping accounts, posting balanced adjustments and reviewing financial statements.

  • CSV and Excel trial-balance imports with account mapping
  • Balanced adjusting journals and opening-equity reconciliation
  • Draft financial statements with PDF, Excel and CSV exports

Subject & technology

What it demonstrates

Financial reporting · reconciliation · validation design.

Project stack: React, TypeScript, decimal.js, Firebase.

Evidence & scope

Verification and limitations

Source and verification documentation inspected. The repository records 36 unit tests and 12 accounting scenarios; those suites were not rerun as part of this portfolio redesign.

Synthetic demo data. Reports remain drafts. Firebase is optional and is not configured in the public demo. Cash-flow reporting requires comparative balances and supplementary movements.
