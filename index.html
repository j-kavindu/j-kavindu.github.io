02 / About me

Numbers give me
a starting point.
Building takes them further.

I’m Kavindu, an accounting undergraduate at the University of Sri Jayewardenepura with an interest in how financial ideas become useful digital tools.

My work explores financial reporting, inventory costing, budgeting, portfolio record keeping and economic decision-making. I use spreadsheets and web applications to make these topics easier to examine and understand.

These are personal and educational projects, including AI-assisted implementation. I focus on understanding the calculations, documenting the assumptions and being clear about what has — and has not — been tested.

I’m interested in internship opportunities, accounting and finance learning, and thoughtful collaboration at the intersection of business and technology.
